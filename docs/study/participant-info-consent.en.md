# Participant information and consent form (English reference version)

*Study: Expert evaluation of audit evidence from AI-agent telemetry
("The Auditor's Trace"). The administered version is German
(participant-info-consent.de.md); this English version is canonical for the
published research artifact. Version 0.1 — draft, to be reviewed by the
regulatory co-author before first use.*

---

## Part 1 — Participant information

**What is this about?** We research how reproducible, article-level audit
evidence under the EU AI Act can be generated from the execution logs of AI
agents (concretely: LLM agents in a simulated credit-scoring process). Your
practitioner perspective from internal audit, model risk, or compliance helps
us judge whether our simulated violation scenarios are realistic and whether
the generated evidence records would be comprehensible and defensible for
audit purposes.

**Who conducts the study?** [Alina Hafner, institution] (study lead) and
Aziz Ketata (technical facilitation). Participation is personal and
voluntary; your employer is not involved, and no information about your
employer or its clients is collected. Introducing contacts (e.g. professional
networks) have no role in design, analysis, or reporting.

**What does participation involve?** Up to two remote sessions:

1. **Catalogue realism review** (~30 min): you rate eight simulated
   violation scenarios on a short structured instrument (plausibility,
   frequency, severity) and name failure modes you consider missing.
2. **Evidence-record walkthrough** (~45–60 min): you attempt to independently
   re-derive a violation from an evidence record and a log excerpt
   (think-aloud), and judge whether the record would survive challenge.

There are no right or wrong answers; the artifact is being evaluated, not
you. Only simulated data is used.

**Voluntariness.** Participation is voluntary and unpaid. You may stop at any
time without giving reasons and withdraw consent until publication; your data
will then be deleted or not used.

**Data protection (GDPR).** Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Audio
recording only with consent; transcripts pseudonymised at creation; audio
deleted after transcription; the pseudonym key stored separately and deleted
at project end; retention of pseudonymised transcripts: [period]. No names or
employer names in any publication; roles described generically; statements
paraphrased or aggregated; verbatim quotes only if individually approved by
you in advance. Anonymised results are used in (a) the scientific
publication, (b) conference presentations (including a talk at Munich Tech
Expo, 20 Sep 2026), and (c) published research artifacts. Rights under
Arts. 15–21 GDPR and the right to complain to a supervisory authority.
Contact: [study-lead email].

**Ethics.** Expert interviews on professional judgments without collection of
sensitive personal data; a documented ethics self-assessment is filed with
the study materials.
